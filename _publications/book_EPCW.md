## Introduction to Electric Power Conversion - Exercise Workbook
This workbook introduces electric power conversion—central to modern electrical
engineering and the energy transition—through a hands-on, beginner-friendly
approach. It offers exercises that involve drawing graphs, performing hand
calculations, and building diagrams, complemented by computer simulations.
Assuming prior knowledge of circuit analysis, it focuses on fundamental
power-electronics (static conversion) configurations and uses PLECS for
numerical work, with a brief appendix to help you get started.

The workbook with the exercise is distributed under the Creative Commons
license and can be downloaded
<a href='https://luigialberti.github.io/files/EPCworkbook_free.pdf'>here.</a>

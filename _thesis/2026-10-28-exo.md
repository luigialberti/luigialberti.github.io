This thesis deals with the position control of a rehabilitation exoskeleton
driven by four electric drives.
Each leg is composed by two motors that have to work in conjunction to
realize the target trajectory.

Rehabilitation exoskeletons help patients recover their movements through repeated and
well controlled exercises, and the quality of the therapy depends largely on how the joint
drives are controlled. The work starts from the implementation of the torque control of
each drive on the dedicated microcontroller.
A model of the complete system is then developed and used to design a trajectory
controller that coordinates the four drives, so that the
exoskeleton follows the desired motion.

Some preliminary work on the subject is already available.

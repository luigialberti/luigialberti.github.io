This thesis explores the use of artificial intelligence techniques to build accurate models
of the magnetic behavior of synchronous machines, which are then validated by finite element
analysis and by measurements.

An accurate magnetic model, typically expressed by the flux linkage maps of the machine, is
the basis for both the design of the motor and the control of the drive. The models obtained
with AI techniques will be compared with the results of finite element simulations,
considering both commercial and open source software, so as to also highlight the
differences between the tools. A final experimental validation will be carried out on the
laboratory test bench.

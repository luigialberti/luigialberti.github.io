This thesis covers the analysis and implementation of a speed observer for an induction
motor, used to identify the mechanical load connected to the shaft and to detect possible
fault conditions.

The speed estimated by the observer carries useful information about the load, and its
harmonic spectrum may reveal changes that are related to faults or dangerous operating
conditions in the load/transmission.
The work includes the study and implementation of the observer,
followed by the spectral analysis of the estimated quantities to recognize the signatures
of anomalous operating conditions of the load.
